Where to paste it

Pick your assistant.

Claude — works on the free plan

Free accounts can add one custom connector. Checked against Claude's help centre on 7 October 2026.

  1. Open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Click + Add, then Add custom connector.
  3. Name it EuroLeague Analytics, paste the address, click Continue, then Add.
  4. Sign in with Google when Claude asks.
  5. In a chat, open + → Connectors and switch it on.

Works on the web, the desktop app and the phone. In Claude Code:

claude mcp add --transport http euroleague https://euroleague-analytics-mcp.fly.dev/mcp

ChatGPT — paid plans, on the web

Custom connectors need Developer mode. Menu names have moved more than once; checked on 7 October 2026.

  1. Open Settings → Security and login and switch on Developer mode.
  2. Go to Settings → Plugins (on some accounts Apps & Connectors) and click + or Create.
  3. Name it European Basketball Analytics, paste the address and choose OAuth.
  4. Sign in with Google, then switch the connector on in a chat.

Gemini — with Gemini Spark

Needs Google AI Pro or Ultra on a personal account; the free Gemini app does not take custom connectors yet. Checked on 6 September 2026.

  1. Open Settings & help → Connected apps → Custom apps for Spark → Add a custom app.
  2. Name it EuroLeague Analytics, paste the address and sign in with Google.
  3. In any chat, type @EuroLeague Analytics and ask.

On a free plan, the shortest way in is Claude.

Why you can trust the answers.

Counted, not estimated
Most stats sites estimate possessions with a formula on the box score. Here every possession is counted from the play-by-play, one at a time, and pace, ratings and lineups rest on that count.
Checked against the official box score
All 732 games of the last two seasons match their official final score, and player minutes match to the second in 99.54% of player games.
Honest about thin samples
A five-man lineup that played 30 possessions is noise. Every lineup answer reaches your assistant with that warning attached, so it tells you too.
Read-only, and open
It answers questions and can't change anything. The code and the tests behind every number are public.

Questions people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. No plan, no card. Each account has a daily fair-use limit, which is what keeps the server affordable to run for everyone.

Is this the official EuroLeague?

No. It is an independent project built from the league's public play-by-play feed. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Euroleague Basketball.

Which seasons can I ask about?

The 2024–25 and 2025–26 EuroLeague seasons in full, and 2026–27 as it is played. Older seasons are archived but not yet open to questions.

Does it work in my language?

Ask in whatever language your assistant speaks, Turkish, Spanish or Greek included. The numbers are the same.

What do you learn about me?

Signing in with Google gives the server an account identifier, used only to apply the daily limit. This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. The privacy policy has the detail.

Who built it?

Egemen Yücelen, as an open-source project. The source, the tests and every decision behind them are on GitHub.

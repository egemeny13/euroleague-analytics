European Basketball Analytics

An independent analytics interface for European professional basketball data, including EuroLeague and EuroCup coverage.

Possessions
Exact play-by-play possession counts and team efficiency metrics.
Lineups
Five-player lineup and on/off analysis with possession context.
Shots
Court shot locations and outcomes for supported competitions.

Independent analytics project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Euroleague Basketball or its competitions.
